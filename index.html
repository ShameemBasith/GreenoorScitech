Lead offer

Direct recycling of lithium-ion batteries

Instead of breaking a battery down to raw metals, Greenoor repairs and regenerates the cathode material itself.

1

Spent LFP battery

End-of-life lithium iron phosphate batteries arrive from manufacturers and e-waste streams.

2

Dismantling & separation

Cells are dismantled and the cathode material is separated from the other components.

3

Re-lithiation

Lithium lost during use is restored, so the cathode is repaired rather than rebuilt.

4

Regenerated cathode

The regenerated material is ready to go back into new batteries.

Why direct recycling

Economical. Less chemical processing than pulling out metals one by one.

Environmentally green. The cathode structure is kept, not rebuilt from scratch.

Sustainable. Spent batteries become the raw material for the next ones.
